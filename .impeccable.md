## Design Context

### Users

AI application developers inspecting LLM reliability and latency; recruiters exploring a fully working read-only demo.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, trustworthy. Data integrity over decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark-first professional observability console, neutral graphite surfaces, subtle borders and a muted warm accent. No gradients or decorative animation. Operational density with readable tables and clearly labeled charts.

### Design Principles

- All displayed metrics must come from the API and disclose simulated data.
- P95, filtering and trace investigation are primary tasks.
- Keyboard-friendly controls, meaningful empty/error/loading states.
- Never imply model quality or a distributed trace tree.
